I actually think this is a good opportunity to differentiate OpsGenie.

**Don't build a typical SaaS website.** Your users are distributors, not software buyers. They don't care about dashboards, AI buzzwords, or feature grids. They care about one question:

> **"Will this stop me from losing money?"**

The website should feel like **a business assistant for distributors**, not a software product.

# The biggest thing I would change

Right now, your website likely feels like **"sign up for software."**

I'd reposition it to sell a transformation:

* **Current state:** "We have an AI assistant."
* **Better:** "Know every morning who will pay you, what you owe suppliers, and whether you'll run out of cash."

If every section reinforces that promise, you'll have a website that feels like a product solving a real business problem rather than another AI tool. That's a much stronger foundation for convincing distributors to trust OpsGenie.
