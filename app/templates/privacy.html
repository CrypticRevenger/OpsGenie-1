Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026

What we collect

When you sign up for OpsGenie, we collect your business name, owner name, WhatsApp number, email, and basic business details (business type, city, GST number if provided). Once your account is active, we process the business data you send us through WhatsApp — invoices, payments, dealers, suppliers — solely to run your daily briefing and reports.

How we use it

Your data is used only to operate OpsGenie for your business: computing cash flow, tracking receivables and payables, sending your morning briefing, and replying to your WhatsApp queries. We do not sell your data.

WhatsApp

OpsGenie communicates with you through the WhatsApp Business Platform (Meta). Messages you send us are processed to route your requests and generate replies.

Data retention

We retain your business data for as long as your account is active, so your historical reports and briefings stay accurate. You can request deletion at any time via Contact.

Contact

Questions about this policy? Reach out via Contact.
